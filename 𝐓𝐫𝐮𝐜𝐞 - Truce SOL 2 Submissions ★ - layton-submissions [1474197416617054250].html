KARMA @Judges ⋅⋅ ♪

-# https://open.spotify.com/track/2skZSmKuBgg37tMT2JnZUn?si=5825a2146f8649aa Karma, my favorite AJR song (prolly.) I had to use AJR for the sadness theme, because they were the band I listened to the most when I was at my lowest. Karma was the song I related to the most. It's a very raw and emotional song To be honest, I tried for like an hour to write a super cool description, and I think a simpler one will work more. This song is structured like a therapy session, in which the singer complains that no matter how hard he works, or how helpful he is, he still is feeling lonely, depressed, and mainly: Empty. Emptiness is the way I experience sadness. The feeling of hollowness; that you are just a cog in a machine called life. The singer doesn't even know why his life is becoming so empty, especially when he seems to be doing everything he is supposed to. In the end, he basically comes to the realization that Karma doesn't exist (which it doesn't,) and asks his therapist if he should be good this year. The song ends rather ambiguous, so you can take it in anyway you want. Even though this theme is sadness, I personally think the singer kept doing good things. As hard as it is, I think its important to do good things and be a good person. Maybe the singer doesn't see it, but there is always light at the end of the tunnel, no matter how empty you feel.
