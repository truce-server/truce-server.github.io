Dumbbell [S1 - Winner] 2025-11-28 3:44 PM

Closing Statement

Yall, it's been a fun time. It's been a long and dynamic game and it's bittersweet that it's coming to a close. But ultimately, I wanna leave one final case for you guys to prove why I'm deserving of a win. Throughout this FTC, I feel like I've been very underestimated by you guys on the jury. Yall saw me as playing passively and for someone else. But I hope I was able to prove the influence I had over this game and how I worked hard to run the numbers and manage my social game to where I actively set the game up to ultimately leave me standing on top. I feel like I was the most unexpected player coming into this finale, and I hope I surprised you all with the influence I had over the moves in this game. In the premerge, I was the person who spearheaded the Yam and Epoch votes, two of the biggest moves of the season, and I did not let that momentum go to waste once the merge rolled around. I was the one who kicked off the Leah vote, putting me in a power position for the rest of the game, leaving Roy and Mouse without much agency from then on. Ever since then, I rode the middle with Zan, and was careful to let him take the sole credit for moves that benefitted me equally, while setting up the playing field to take out the threats to my game systematically. After Hunter and Blake went, me and Zan reached an impasse where what benefitted me was not what benefitted him. But ultimately due to my social game and the loyalty Zan had to me, he bent to my agenda proving that I was not his follower, and took out Remi at 7, leaving me to win immunity at 6 to spearhead the move against Shadow due to having saved up my Heathfire Ward to use on Rodeo, despite them not being what Zan admitted to being his most optimal plays. And ultimately, through a combination of my social connections, strategic planning, and competitive prowess, I was able to lock my position at the end with Mouse and Roy following that move. (edited)
3:44 PM
I truly believe that I was the snake in the grass in this game. Always in the prime position to take a shot, but waited for the most apt time to make the moves in this game, working hard to slither away quickly to avoid getting caught. I didn’t do too much to put a target on myself, but always did enough to get the result that ultimately most benefitted me. I had a great understanding for timing and execution when it came to the effectiveness of a plan, and I do believe I have the resume to show for it. I balanced big moves and threat management the best out of anyone on this cast, and showed this game so much heart and dedication, and I truly think I managed to Outwit, Outplay, and Outlast the best of the opponents I’m sitting next to. I made moves, caused chaos, bonded a lot with you guys, played a loyal game while still being cutthroat and aggressive, and ultimately, played a game I am truly proud of. During Touchy Subjects, I was voted the Dark Horse to win this season. In other words, the person who had the potential to win if the unexpected route for the game ended up occurring. Well, I hope that I surprised you all with the social influence I had and the thorough strategic planning that I implemented throughout the game. I was not Zan’s plus one, I was not a passive player in this game, and I hope the moves that I have explained in detail throughout this finale proves that to you guys. I’m not going to sit here and pretend that my game was perfect because it was not, but I do believe I have the strongest and most deserving game to win. I was consistently in a power position, and used it to set up this Final 3, dragging Mouse and Roy kicking and screaming here when they never wanted to take me in the first place, and I think that truly displays who had the most agency amongst us.
