English Translation here Underwater by Red Velvet is a song that likens water to love in many ways. It’s one of the most simple analogies to make, as love and water are both seen as nourishing, ever-present facets of life, however the depiction of the narrator’s love is one that is so excessive and obsessive that it’s implied that they drown their lover in it. The song itself particularly excels in portraying different types of love, specifically in terms of Ancient Greek paradigms of love. The quite obvious portrayal of Mania (obsessive love) and Érōs (romantic/sexual love) specifically in asking their lover to never leave and the intimacy of wanting to be close and enveloped with their lover, using words associated with water to portray this feeling, being filled to the edge, ingrained, sinking deep into, and even flowing in are images that portray the image of love and devotion so intense that you want to remove the boundary between oneself and your beloved, showing how despite water being naturally sustaining and gives life, it is also perilous and dangerous. Love is something that sustains you, but it is also something that can drown you in its depths, one you can observe in the absence of Agápē (selfless love) in the song. If Érōs + Mania drive the descent underwater, your selfishness will lead to the both of you drowning forever in its endless depth. It’s quite a nice contradiction to traditional meaning of water itself, and it’s one I deeply enjoy as a nerd ass lit major <3 The song in itself is very sensual, Red Velvet is a group that excels specifically in RNB, I think the way they sing allows you to feel the sensations of love even without looking at the translation. They frequently mention a shivering, cold feeling, searching for warmth from their love, and I think the way they sing excellently does that.
