SUBMISSION 1 - "She's My Collar (feat. Kali Uchis)" - Gorillaz

Love can take on many forms, hence when you get something so broad as this I aim to take a different route than usual. Roses are a common symbol of love, you may provide some significant other with a bouquet of roses, elegant petals that signify an undying and indisputable love for another, however many roses fail to have their thorns plucked. She's My Collar is not a traditional love song that demonstrates the commonly painted picture of the elegance and beauty that many relationships on the surface appear to be. She's My Collar explores the toxicity of love by exploring the story of someone who is obsessed with another. Meanwhile this person knows they hold this particular feeling and abuses it for their own personal gain to keep them complicit. This is shown via the start of the song describing this person in control being "my collar" and how the protagonist "needs no other". Then by the middle of the song, the protagonist wants to flee wanting "to taste another" but being pulled back from "She's the first I'm running with" being changed after the collar is collected to then "She's the running with" being repeated as if its some sort of re-assurance after the thought of escaping comes along. Shes My Collar explores an unhealthy love, and is definetly an amazing 'love' song. (edited)

SUBMISSION 2 (Anger) - "Crying Lightning" - Artic Monkeys

Anger is an ugly emotion, it hurts not just the self for its experience but also those who surround us. So when it comes down to utilising what songs I have with the 3 artists I picked, Crying Lightning is the only plausible submission that one could take. This song explores an anger being brought to the surface via someone elses actions, an anger slowly built up over time that is both relatable and revealing. The song opens with this guitar that follows the song through every chorus, symbolising a dormant anger that eventually gets more weight as various other tracks stack on top of it. The lyrics back this anger to another, with direction based lines like "I hate that little game you called..." and "the way you aggrevate the icky man on rainy afternoons". The term 'Crying Lightning' is also poetic in anger with it turning a sigil for sadness into an outward and destructive martyr for anger. Crying Lightning depicts anger in the context of an individuals disposure for another, and how over time feeding into that rage will result in only destructive feedback.
